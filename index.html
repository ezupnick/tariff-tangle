Clear the tariffs

Striped tariff gates can't be matched or moved, and they tax everything you ship, which means higher prices for families over the holidays. The only way to clear them is to reach a US-Canada trade deal.

Fact-based. The goods, routes and price effects are drawn from sources like the USDA, EIA, USTR, Statistics Canada, the Yale Budget Lab and the Tax Foundation. In-game prices are illustrative; the real figures appear on your end-of-level receipt.
