# Tariff Tangle

A US-Canada holiday trade game, built for the Canadian American Business Council (CABC).

Tariff Tangle is a festive match-and-build puzzle that shows, through play, how deeply the US and
Canadian economies share the holidays, and how tariffs flow into the prices families and businesses pay.
It is fact-based.

## Play it

Open `index.html` in any modern browser. It is a single self-contained file (no build step, no server,
no dependencies beyond Google Fonts). It works on desktop and mobile, in light and dark mode.

To host it for free, drop it on any static host or enable GitHub Pages on this repo and point it at the
root. Rename nothing: `index.html` is the entry point.

## How it works

- **Match to build.** Match three of the same tile and they combine into the next stage of the supply
  chain: raw material, then part, then finished good. That progression is the real journey goods take.
- **Ship for points.** A finished good ships to your Holiday Cart with a price. Fill the shopping list
  before you run out of moves.
- **Clear the tariffs.** Striped tariff gates add a surcharge to anything you ship while they are on the
  board. Clear them with adjacent matches, or bank a Trade Deal to wipe them all at once.
- **Affordability.** The meter and the end-of-level receipt show what the tariffs cost you, with a
  side-by-side of what you paid versus what a deal would have cost.

Three levels carry the season from Thanksgiving through Christmas to New Year's.

## Framing

Tariffs are a game obstacle, and the win state is a durable US-Canada deal with lower, more certain
prices for both countries. In-game prices are illustrative and scaled; the sourced facts appear on each
level's receipt.

## The numbers

Every fact in the game is grounded in a source. See `FACT_SHEET.md` for the full research: figures,
attributions, URLs, confidence levels, and a pre-launch verification checklist. The live tariff landscape
is changing week to week, so any rate in a public launch should be re-verified against primary sources
first.
